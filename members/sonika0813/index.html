About me

Sunlight zone, 0–200 m

I’m Sonika, a second-year Computer Science and Engineering student at RVCE who gets happiest when a half-broken idea finally runs.

I’m drawn to problems where something is missing: a damaged image, a confusing piece of code, or the temperature of water no satellite can see. The job is to work out what belongs there and build a system that can do it reliably.

OceanEmbed-NIO

Deep learning on satellite data

?

Try it: a satellite only sees the surface. Drag the depth, then reconstruct what’s below.

The problem

Satellites can only observe the ocean’s surface, but a lot of what matters, like heat stored below, happens at depth.

The idea

Use satellite embeddings, which are compact numerical summaries of surface data, and a deep learning framework to reconstruct subsurface ocean temperature.

What I built

I trained the temperature models in Google Colab and combined two of them, versions 3 and 4, into an ensemble that leans toward version 4. A FastAPI backend serves them to a React dashboard with Explore, Sections and Validate views. I’m now adding marine heat events and cyclone thermal context using IBTrACS storm-track data.

How it works

  1. Collect surface satellite data.
  2. Turn it into embeddings.
  3. Train deep models to predict temperature below the surface.
  4. Combine two trained models into an ensemble.
  5. Serve the results through a FastAPI and React dashboard.

Built with
